The downloadable builds (the Windows installer and the Mac app) also carry these programs and
libraries, each under its own license. All of them are free and open source; their source code is
available at the addresses below.

Programs
- FFmpeg and FFprobe, GPL 3.0 builds. Source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
  Windows build: https://github.com/yt-dlp/FFmpeg-Builds (build scripts and sources)
  Mac build: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de (build scripts and sources)
- Deno, MIT. https://github.com/denoland/deno

Libraries
- yt-dlp, Unlicense. https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
- yt-dlp-ejs, Unlicense. https://github.com/yt-dlp/ejs
- Python, PSF License. https://www.python.org
- PyInstaller bootloader, GPL 2.0 with an exception that allows any program built with it.
  https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller
- rich, MIT. https://github.com/Textualize/rich
- requests, Apache 2.0. https://github.com/psf/requests
- urllib3, MIT. https://github.com/urllib3/urllib3
- certifi, MPL 2.0. https://github.com/certifi/python-certifi
- websockets, BSD 3-Clause. https://github.com/python-websockets/websockets
- brotli, MIT. https://github.com/google/brotli
- pycryptodomex, BSD 2-Clause and public domain. https://github.com/Legrandin/pycryptodome

Windows only
- Tcl/Tk, BSD-style license. https://www.tcl-lang.org
- Sun Valley ttk theme (sv-ttk), MIT. https://github.com/rdbende/Sun-Valley-ttk-theme
- darkdetect, BSD 3-Clause. https://github.com/albertosottile/darkdetect
- Pillow, MIT-CMU. https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow

FFmpeg and FFprobe are separate programs that vidkeep runs; they are distributed alongside vidkeep
under their own GPL 3.0 license, which lets you use, share and change them freely. That license
covers those two programs only, not vidkeep.
